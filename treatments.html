Biomarker-Driven Care

Precision Oncology

Personalised treatment planning informed by the individual characteristics of the cancer.

For selected advanced or metastatic cancers, molecular information and Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) reports may help inform treatment decisions.

The Precision Oncology Pathway

1

Cancer Diagnosis

Accurate histopathology, staging scans, and comprehensive clinical evaluation.

2

Molecular / Genomic Information

Targeted biomarker analysis or NGS genomic sequencing when clinically appropriate.

3

Treatment Planning

Evidence-based therapy selection matched to actionable molecular targets.

4

Personalised Care

Targeted therapy execution, tolerability monitoring, and continuous clinical support.

Clinical Guidance: Precision oncology and genomic testing are evaluated on an individual case-by-case basis. They are not required for every cancer stage or patient, nor do they guarantee a specific treatment outcome.
