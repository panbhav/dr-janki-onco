Daycare Systemic Therapy

Chemotherapy

Daycare Infusion (Zero Overnight Stay)

Core Mechanism of Action

Chemotherapy utilizes powerful cytotoxic drugs delivered systemically into the bloodstream to target and destroy rapidly dividing cancer cells, prevent tumor recurrence after surgery (adjuvant), or shrink tumors beforehand (neoadjuvant).

Patient Experience & Tolerability

Administered with modern anti-nausea premedications (5-HT3 and NK1 receptor antagonists), cold-cap scalp cooling coordination, and painless Chemoport access for maximum comfort.

Primary Clinical Uses

Breast, Lung, Colorectal, Ovarian, Head & Neck cancers, Sarcomas, and Lymphomas across curative and palliative settings.

*Treatment protocols are customized strictly based on patient organ function, stage, and histology.
