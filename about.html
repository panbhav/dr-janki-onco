Clinical Research Projects

Research Project 01 Head & Neck Oncology

Serum Albumin Levels as Predictive Marker in Locally Advanced Head and Neck Cancer Treated with Concomitant Chemo-Radiation

Investigated baseline and nadir serum albumin levels as an objective predictive bio-marker for acute toxicities, systemic nutritional reserve, and locoregional treatment completion rates in patients receiving definitive concurrent chemo-radiotherapy protocols.

Research Project 02 / DNB Thesis Breast Medical Oncology

Clinical & Pathological Response of Neo-Adjuvant Chemotherapy Regimens in Triple Negative Breast Cancer With Addition of Platinum as Per Current Standard

Comprehensive DNB medical oncology research evaluating pathological complete response (pCR) rates, hematological tolerability, and event-free outcomes in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC) treated with contemporary platinum-incorporating neoadjuvant systemic chemotherapy protocols.

Presented at Indraprastha Apollo Hospitals (CBCS)
